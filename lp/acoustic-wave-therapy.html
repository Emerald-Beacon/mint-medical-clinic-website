Common questions

Questions, answered.

Does Acoustic Wave Therapy actually work?
Yes — for the right patient. Multiple meta-analyses show low-intensity shockwave therapy (Li-ESWT/AWT) produces clinically significant improvement in erectile function, with response rates around 75% in men with vasculogenic ED. Your initial evaluation determines whether you're a strong candidate.
Is it painful?
No. Sessions are low-intensity and most men describe them as a mild tapping sensation. No anesthesia is required, and you can drive yourself home or back to work immediately.
How many sessions will I need?
Most patients complete 6–12 sessions over 4–6 weeks, depending on severity and response. Your custom protocol is built around your evaluation — we don't run cookie-cutter programs.
How long do results last?
Many men experience sustained improvement for 12 months or longer, with some studies showing benefits up to two years post-treatment. Lifestyle factors (sleep, cardiovascular health, hormones) affect durability — we'll cover all of that in your visit.
Can AWT replace my ED medication?
For many men, yes. The goal is to restore natural function so pills become unnecessary — or significantly less needed. Some men use lower-dose PDE5 medication as a bridge while AWT results build.
Is there any downtime or recovery?
None. AWT is non-invasive — no surgery, no needles, no anesthesia. You walk in, complete your 20-minute session, and walk out. Most men return immediately to work, the gym, or daily life.
How much does it cost?
Pricing depends on your protocol length and treatment plan. Your consultation covers all pricing transparently, and we offer financing through Cherry and LiteCredit with fast soft-credit approvals so you can start without delay.
Is the consultation really free?
Yes. Your initial consultation at Mint Medical Clinic is completely complimentary. No card on file, no obligation. You'll meet with our team, get real answers, and decide what's next on your own terms.
