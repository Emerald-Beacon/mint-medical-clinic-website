Common questions

Questions, answered.

How much weight can I expect to lose?
In clinical trials, patients on Tirzepatide lost an average of 20–25% of their body weight (about 52 lbs over 72 weeks at the highest dose). Semaglutide patients lost an average of 15–17%. Your results depend on starting weight, dose, lifestyle, and individual response — which is why your physician's protocol matters.
Tirzepatide or Semaglutide — which is right for me?
Both are excellent. Tirzepatide tends to produce greater weight loss thanks to its dual GLP-1 + GIP mechanism. Semaglutide has a longer track record and is highly effective for most patients. We'll help you choose based on your goals, medical history, tolerability, and preferences during your free consultation.
What are the side effects?
The most common side effects are mild GI symptoms — nausea, constipation, or fullness — typically when starting or increasing your dose. They generally improve over time. Our gradual titration approach is specifically designed to minimize these effects.
Am I a candidate for GLP-1 therapy?
GLP-1 therapy may be appropriate for adults with a BMI of 30 or higher, or 27+ with a weight-related condition. It's not recommended for patients with a personal or family history of medullary thyroid cancer, MEN 2 syndrome, or pancreatitis. Your medical evaluation will determine the right fit.
How is the medication administered?
Once-weekly subcutaneous injection — quick, simple, and self-administered at home. We provide full instruction during your visit and you'll be confident before you leave.

Is the consultation really free?
Yes. Your initial consultation at Mint Medical Clinic is completely complimentary. No card on file, no obligation. You'll meet with our team, get real answers, and decide what's next on your own terms.
How quickly will I see results?
Most patients begin to notice reduced appetite and food noise within the first 1–2 weeks. Visible weight loss typically begins in the first month and accelerates as your dose is titrated upward over the following weeks.
