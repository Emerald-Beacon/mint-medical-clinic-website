Please Read

Important safety information

The Opti-Mint Shot is a prescription product compounded by a licensed pharmacy. It's appropriate for many healthy adults, but it isn't right for everyone — which is exactly why a provider reviews every order.

Never combine it with nitrate medications such as nitroglycerin; the combination can cause a dangerous drop in blood pressure. Do not mix it with alcohol. It may not be appropriate if you have certain heart conditions, uncontrolled high or low blood pressure, a history of stroke, significant liver or kidney disease, or if you are pregnant, trying to become pregnant, or breastfeeding.

Possible side effects include nausea, flushing, headache, dizziness, and temporary changes in blood pressure. Seek medical attention right away for an erection lasting more than four hours, chest pain, fainting, or a sudden change in vision or hearing.

Compounded formulations are not FDA-approved as a combination product. Individual results vary. Tell your provider about every medication and supplement you take. This page is not medical advice and does not replace guidance from your provider.
