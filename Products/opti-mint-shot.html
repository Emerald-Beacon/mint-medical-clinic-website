Important Safety Information

The Opti-Mint Shot is appropriate for many healthy adults, but it isn't right for everyone. Never combine it with nitrate medications such as nitroglycerin — the combination can cause a dangerous drop in blood pressure. Do not mix it with alcohol. It may not be appropriate if you have certain heart conditions, uncontrolled high or low blood pressure, a history of stroke, significant liver or kidney disease, or if you are pregnant, trying to become pregnant, or breastfeeding.

Possible side effects include nausea, flushing, headache, dizziness, and temporary changes in blood pressure. Seek medical attention right away for an erection lasting more than four hours, chest pain, fainting, or a sudden change in vision or hearing.

This is a compounded prescription product. Compounded formulations are not FDA-approved as a combination, and individual results vary. Tell your provider about every medication and supplement you take. This information does not replace medical advice from your provider.
