Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What are Mint Mints?

Mint Mints are physician-prescribed, fast-acting lozenges (troches) for erectile dysfunction. They contain proven ED medications such as sildenafil or tadalafil in a mint-flavored lozenge that dissolves under the tongue, so the medication absorbs directly into the bloodstream for a quicker, more discreet experience than swallowing a traditional pill.

How are Mint Mints different from BlueChew or Hims?

Like BlueChew and Hims, Mint Mints are prescribed online and shipped discreetly. The difference is that Mint Mints come from a real Utah-based clinic you can actually visit, with no rigid subscription lock-in. Because they're sublingual lozenges rather than chewables or pills, many patients experience faster onset, and your physician personalizes the strength to you.

How fast do Mint Mints work?

Because Mint Mints dissolve under the tongue and absorb through the tissues of the mouth, many patients notice effects faster than with a swallowed pill, often within 15 to 30 minutes. Onset varies by individual, formulation, and dosage, which your physician will discuss with you.

Do I need a prescription?

Yes. Mint Mints contain FDA-approved ED medications and require a prescription. Getting one is simple: complete a quick, confidential telehealth consultation with a Mint Medical Clinic provider. If Mint Mints are right for you, your prescription ships discreetly to your door.

How do I take Mint Mints?

Place one lozenge under your tongue and let it fully dissolve. Do not chew or swallow it whole. Take it ahead of intimacy as directed by your provider. Because the medication absorbs sublingually, you can take it discreetly without water.

Are Mint Mints safe?

Mint Mints use the same clinically proven PDE5-inhibitor medications found in Viagra and Cialis. They're safe for most healthy men, but not appropriate for everyone, especially men taking nitrates or with certain heart conditions. That's why every order includes a medical review by a licensed physician before anything is prescribed.
