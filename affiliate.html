Program Terms at a Glance

The essentials from the Affiliate Services Agreement. The signed agreement is what governs — this is the plain-English summary.

Free GLP-1 Product After 10 Units

Once you've generated your first 10 approved sales (counted across all GLP-1 products), you become eligible for free GLP-1 product in addition to your cash commissions. The product, dosage, quantity, and cadence are determined by the clinic, and it's subject to our standard clinical intake, eligibility screening, and approval by a licensed provider.

GLP-1 Exclusivity

While you're in the program, you promote GLP-1 products exclusively for Mint Medical Clinic. That means no promoting, endorsing, or referring for any other provider that sells or prescribes GLP-1 or competing weight-management medications — including your own brands and social channels. Disclose any existing arrangement up front and end it before you start.

Monthly Payment

Commissions are calculated and paid monthly, within 30 days after the close of each period, for all sales that became approved during that period. You're responsible for providing accurate payment and tax information, and for all taxes on what you earn. We issue a 1099 where the law requires it.

Chargebacks & Adjustments

If a sale is refunded, charged back, cancelled, or returned after we've already paid you the commission, we may deduct that amount from a future commission payment or invoice you for it. No commission is payable on quotes, unpaid orders, or orders declined for clinical or other reasons.

Independent Contractor

You're an independent contractor, not an employee. Nothing in the program creates an employment, partnership, joint venture, agency, or franchise relationship. You're responsible for your own expenses, taxes, and insurance.

Term & Termination

The agreement runs until either side ends it. Either party can terminate for convenience on 15 days' written notice. We can terminate immediately for a breach, a violation of law, or conduct that could harm the clinic or its patients. Commissions earned before termination are still paid in the ordinary course.

What We Ask of Affiliates

GLP-1 medications are prescription products, so their promotion carries real legal and regulatory obligations. These aren't optional.

  • Promote lawfully and truthfully, using only marketing materials and claims we've approved in writing.
  • Make no false, misleading, or unsubstantiated claims about the products, their safety, efficacy, or results — and never guarantee a medical outcome.
  • Comply with all federal, state, and local laws covering advertising, telehealth, consumer protection, privacy, and the marketing of prescription products.
  • Don't give medical advice, diagnose, or represent yourself as a licensed healthcare provider unless you actually are one and are acting within your scope.
  • Use only the tracking links, referral codes, and attribution methods we approve, so approved sales can be verified and credited to you.
  • No spam, deceptive advertising, trademark bidding, or anything that could damage the clinic's reputation or violate a platform's terms.
  • Keep our non-public information confidential — patient data, pricing, business methods, and marketing materials — and follow all applicable privacy laws.
